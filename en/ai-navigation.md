# AI Navigation

## Navigating an AI-Enabled Environment

In the OECUMENE model, AI Navigation is a discipline at the level:

**WHAT? · What?**

WHAT asks:

> What emerges as a result of realizing WHY through the architecture, methods, and practices of HOW?

AI Navigation is the result of developing the system's ability to find direction in an AI-enabled environment and determine meaningful next steps under conditions of uncertainty.

# WHY AI Navigation

The development of artificial intelligence creates a rapidly changing environment of technologies, capabilities, risks, opportunities, and new ways of working.

This creates new questions:

- where to look;
- what to explore;
- what to question;
- what to understand;
- when to act.

AI Navigation asks:

> Why does a system need the ability to find direction in an AI-enabled environment?

---

# HOW AI Navigation

AI Navigation is realized through practices that help a system orient itself, explore possibilities, make sense of information, and determine direction.

This includes:

- orientation;
- framing;
- exploration;
- sensemaking;
- decision making;
- determining direction.

---

# AI Navigation as a Discipline

AI Navigation is a field of knowledge and practice that helps a system develop capabilities for:

- understanding the AI environment;
- identifying relevant questions and opportunities;
- exploring alternatives and implications;
- connecting information, evidence, and perspectives;
- navigating uncertainty;
- determining meaningful next steps.

---

# Core Elements of AI Navigation

## Orientation

Defines:

- what is happening in the AI environment;
- what may be relevant to the system;
- where attention should be directed.

---

## Framing

Defines:

- what the real question is;
- what needs to be understood;
- what assumptions need to be examined.

---

## Exploration

Enables:

- investigation of possibilities;
- comparison of alternatives;
- examination of assumptions;
- exploration of implications.

---

## Sensemaking

Connects:

- information;
- evidence;
- perspectives;
- observations.

The purpose is to develop a meaningful picture of the situation.

---

## Decision Making

Defines:

- what deserves attention;
- what should be explored further;
- what should be experimented with;
- what should be acted upon.

---

## Direction

Determines:

- what the next meaningful move is;
- which path should be explored;
- how to move forward while uncertainty remains.

# Working Position of OECUMENE

AI Navigation is not about predicting the future.

It is the capability of a system to find direction, continuously update its understanding, and determine meaningful next steps while operating under uncertainty.

The objective is not to eliminate uncertainty before acting.

The objective is to improve the quality of orientation, questions, exploration, and decisions while moving through uncertainty.

The central question is:

> **Where should we look, what should we question, and what should we do next?**
