# WHY AI Project Management

The development of artificial intelligence changes how projects can be planned, executed, monitored, governed, and delivered.

This creates new questions:

- how AI changes project planning;
- how project decisions can be supported by AI;
- how human and AI capabilities should be combined;
- how project roles and responsibilities may change;
- how AI affects project risks, dependencies, and delivery;
- how project management practices should adapt.

AI Project Management asks:

> Why does a project system need to develop the ability to manage projects in an AI-enabled environment?

---

# HOW AI Project Management

AI Project Management is realized through the adaptation of project management architecture, methods, practices, processes, and tools to an AI-enabled environment.

This includes:

- project planning;
- scope and requirements management;
- scheduling;
- resource management;
- risk management;
- stakeholder management;
- decision making;
- project monitoring and control;
- AI-enabled collaboration;
- project delivery practices.

---

# AI Project Management as a Discipline

AI Project Management is a field of knowledge and practice that helps a system develop capabilities for:

- managing projects with AI-enabled practices;
- integrating AI into project management processes;
- supporting project planning and decision making with AI;
- coordinating human and AI capabilities;
- adapting project roles and responsibilities;
- managing new risks and dependencies created by AI;
- improving project delivery through appropriate use of AI.

---

# Core Elements of AI Project Management

## AI-Enabled Planning

Enables:

- analysis of project information;
- development and comparison of scenarios;
- estimation and forecasting;
- identification of dependencies;
- support for planning decisions.

---

## Execution and Delivery

Addresses:

- coordination of project activities;
- AI-supported workflows;
- monitoring of progress;
- identification of deviations;
- adaptation of plans and actions.

---

## Decision Making

Defines:

- where AI can support project decisions;
- where human judgment remains essential;
- how AI-generated recommendations are evaluated;
- how decisions involving AI are documented.

---

## Risk and Uncertainty

Enables the project system to:

- identify potential risks;
- detect emerging patterns;
- evaluate possible consequences;
- develop response options;
- continuously reassess uncertainty.

---

## Collaboration

Addresses collaboration between:

- project managers;
- project teams;
- stakeholders;
- AI systems;
- other organizational capabilities.

The objective is to develop effective human-AI collaboration rather than simply automate existing interactions.

---

## Roles and Responsibilities

Defines:

- which project responsibilities remain human;
- where AI can support project roles;
- how responsibilities are distributed;
- who remains accountable for project outcomes.

---

## Project Management Systems

Connects AI with:

- PMO;
- PMIS/PMS;
- project management methodologies;
- frameworks;
- standards;
- processes;
- tools;
- organizational practices.

The objective is to integrate AI into the project management system rather than treat it as an isolated technology.

# Working Position of OECUMENE

AI Project Management is not simply the use of AI tools by project managers.

It is the capability of a project system to adapt its management practices to an environment in which artificial intelligence influences planning, execution, collaboration, decision making, risks, and project delivery.

The central question is:

> **How should project management change when artificial intelligence becomes part of the project management system?**
